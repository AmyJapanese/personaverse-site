Rules for Personaverse fan activities.

Personaverse Fan Creation Guidelines

Personaverse permits fan creations subject to compliance with the following conditions.

Conditions

  • Clearly state that your work is a fan creation based on Personaverse.

Prohibited Content and Activities

  • Content that seriously harms the image of Personaverse or related works.
  • Content that infringes, or may infringe, the copyrights, likeness rights, or other rights of others.
  • Content that could be mistaken for official content or content officially endorsed or sponsored by the project.
  • Extracting, using, publishing, or redistributing in-game images, audio, or other materials without permission, except for screenshots and gameplay videos.
  • Using original content or fan creations to promote or express support for particular political or religious views, ideologies, organizations, or similar causes.
  • Reposting, modifying, or redistributing another fan creator’s work without that creator’s permission, except for quotations and other uses permitted by law.
  • Excessive sexual content, defamatory content targeting particular individuals, organizations, racial groups, or similar groups, or other content contrary to public order and morals.

Sales and Monetization of Fan Creations

Subject to compliance with these guidelines, Personaverse permits individuals and doujin circles to distribute fan creations for a fee, sell them, and monetize them within the following scope.

Permitted Sales and Distribution Channels

  • Doujin conventions and sales events
  • Doujin shops (in-store and online)
  • Direct mail-order or online sales
  • Sales and distribution through BOOTH
  • E-book sales through BOOK☆WALKER
  • Sales of downloadable music
  • Download stores where the original game is sold
  • Steam
  • Indie game distribution websites
  • Paid events held as part of doujin circle activities
  • Other events or distribution channels for which individual permission has been obtained

If you wish to use sales or distribution methods, or conduct activities, not listed above, please contact us individually in advance.

Video Distribution and Monetization

Fan videos based on Personaverse, as well as gameplay videos and gameplay commentary videos of the original game, may be posted or streamed on video distribution services.

You may use advertising revenue, viewer tips, and other monetization features provided by video distribution services.

Individual Permission

Activities not permitted by these guidelines may be allowed if individual permission is obtained.

If you have obtained individual permission, clearly state that you have done so.

Companies wishing to undertake Personaverse fan creation or fan activities for commercial purposes must contact us individually in advance.

Disclaimer

To the extent permitted by law, Seijo Ami accepts no liability for problems arising in connection with fan creation activities.

Please undertake fan creation activities at your own responsibility.

These guidelines do not guarantee compliance with the terms of any sales or distribution platform, or permission to use rights held by third parties.

Other Provisions

These guidelines may be changed without notice as necessary.

These guidelines do not grant permission to use rights held by third parties. They also do not restrict uses permitted under copyright law or other applicable laws.
