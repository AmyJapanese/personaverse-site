What is Personaverse?

Personaverse is a creative series inspired by the histories and cultures of real cities, states, and regions, portraying each as a personified being.

The memories of cities and the distinctive qualities of regions shape the characters, their relationships, and their stories.

The Setting

The series features a variety of cities and regions, primarily across the United States. Its fantasy world draws inspiration from historical events and the unique character of each place.

Salem RPG

An RPG set in the world of Personaverse. Meet the personified beings of different cities and follow the stories of each place.
